Bedside GI calculators

The four scores you'll reach for on call. Inputs follow the original publications; results are educational and don't replace clinical judgment.

Glasgow-Blatchford

Pre-endoscopy risk in upper GI bleeding. Score 0–1 = very low risk (ACG 2021).

MELD 3.0

Mortality/transplant priority in chronic liver disease (Kim et al., 2021). Inputs are clamped per the model.

Child-Pugh

Cirrhosis severity class. Drives prognosis and pre-emptive TIPS logic.

BISAP

Early severity prediction in acute pancreatitis (within 24 h). ≥3 = elevated risk.

Calculators reviewed July 2026. Always confirm inputs and cutoffs against the original publications and your institution's tools before acting.
