Esophagus · Acid

GERD & erosive esophagitis

PRIMARY SOURCES: ACG Guideline for the Diagnosis and Management of GERD (Katz et al., 2022) · Los Angeles classification

GI HandbookEsophagus · GERD→

Bottom line

Classic heartburn and regurgitation without alarm features earns an empiric 8-week once-daily PPI taken 30–60 minutes before the first meal — no scope needed to start. Endoscopy is for alarm features, refractory symptoms, or Barrett's screening. After healing, step down to the lowest effective dose; LA grade C/D esophagitis and Barrett's are the exceptions that stay on PPI and get follow-up.

Endoscope / investigate now
  • Dysphagia, odynophagia, or a food-impaction history — structural disease until proven otherwise
  • GI bleeding, iron-deficiency anemia, or unintentional weight loss
  • Persistent vomiting, or new symptoms at age >60 — don't call it reflux without a look

Diagnose without over-testing

  • Typical symptoms + no alarm features → empiric PPI trial is the diagnostic and therapeutic step.
  • Reserve endoscopy for alarm features, non-response, or Barrett's screening (chronic GERD plus ≥3 risk factors: male, age >50, white, smoking, obesity, family history).
  • Ambulatory reflux monitoring (pH or pH-impedance) confirms or excludes GERD before escalating to surgery or in PPI non-responders — done off PPI when the question is "is this reflux at all," on PPI when the question is "is residual reflux the cause."

  • Optimize timing before escalating dose — most "PPI failures" are mistimed doses taken with or after food.
  • Weight loss and head-of-bed elevation are the lifestyle measures with the best evidence; blanket dietary elimination is not required.

Refractory symptoms

  • Confirm adherence and timing, then confirm the diagnosis with reflux monitoring — a large share of "refractory GERD" is functional heartburn or reflux hypersensitivity, which don't respond to more acid suppression.
  • Consider anti-reflux surgery or magnetic sphincter augmentation only after objective GERD is documented.
  • Don't reflexively stop PPIs over observational-association fears; weigh the documented indication against low-quality risk signals.
Pearls for the note
  • "PPI once daily" is not the same as "PPI before breakfast" — write the timing, because that alone rescues many non-responders.
  • A normal endoscopy does not exclude GERD; it excludes erosive disease and Barrett's.
  • Barrett's with dysplasia is an endoscopic-therapy conversation (ablation/EMR), not just longer PPI.
  • PPI-responsive esophageal eosinophilia is now considered part of the EoE spectrum — persistent dysphagia deserves biopsies even if acid suppression helps.
